## 🌈 Overview

Lighting can completely change the feel of a workspace, and **Halo** was created to bring vibrant ambient lighting into a clean, modern design.

Designed entirely in **Autodesk Fusion 360**, Halo features a compact **3D-printed enclosure** that houses a **Waveshare ESP32-C3 Mini**, a rechargeable **18650 Li-ion battery**, and **Waveshare's newly launched ultra-slim RGB LED strip**. At just **2.7 mm** thick, the LED strip fits perfectly inside the circular diffuser, producing a smooth and uniform lighting effect.

The firmware continuously cycles through the **ROYGBIV rainbow spectrum**, displaying each color for **5 seconds** before transitioning to the next. Powered by a rechargeable battery with **USB Type-C charging**, Halo can be placed anywhere without being limited by power cables, making it ideal for desks, bedrooms, shelves, gaming setups, or decorative lighting.

This repository includes everything needed to recreate the project, including the complete source code, CAD files, STL models, circuit diagram, and step-by-step documentation.

## ⚙️ How It Works

At the heart of Halo is the **Waveshare ESP32-C3 Mini**, programmed using the **Arduino IDE** and **FastLED** library.

When powered on, the ESP32-C3 controls the RGB LED strip and continuously cycles through the **ROYGBIV** rainbow spectrum. Each color remains visible for **5 seconds** before smoothly transitioning to the next, creating a relaxing ambient lighting effect with no user interaction required.

The lamp is powered by a rechargeable **18650 Li-ion battery**, while the integrated **USB Type-C charging module** allows convenient charging without opening the enclosure.

The combination of modern CAD design, 3D printing, embedded programming, and RGB lighting results in a compact desk lamp that looks and feels like a commercial product while remaining fully open for customization.
